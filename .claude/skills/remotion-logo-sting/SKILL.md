# Remotion logo sting

Follow `remotion-setup` first.

1. 4-second composition: logo mask-reveals, a color wipe, then a tagline.
2. Expose duration, colors, logo and tagline as zod props.
3. Export it as a standalone composition that can be dropped into other videos via `<Sequence>`.
4. Verify stills at reveal, wipe and final frames.
